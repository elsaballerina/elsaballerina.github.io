About Me ✨

Hi Everyone! 👋 I am called Elsa Ballerina on social media and I am a proud trans woman, part of the LGBTQIA+ 🏳️‍⚧️ community.

My gaming history is deeply intertwined with PC gaming. I started with classics like Bungie's Marathon on a Power Mac and moved through the era of id Software's Quake and DOOM, not forgetting the intense Sierra Online Half-Life deathmatches.

These games connected me with a community, whether through GameSpy, C3, or Discord. Sure, those cumulative hours could have earned me four PhDs! But while gaming might have been a distraction from academia, it offered something just as valuable: a safe, sane, and far more affordable form of entertainment. 💖
